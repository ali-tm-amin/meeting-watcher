# English + Arabic terms

For calls held in English with Arabic and Islamic terms mixed in. Output is English only; every Arabic term is kept in Latin letters with its closest English meaning in brackets on first use.

## Summary

Summarise the meeting in English. Keep every Arabic word or Islamic term exactly as spoken, written in Latin letters, and add its closest English meaning in brackets the first time it appears, for example "inshallah (God willing)" or "sadaqah (voluntary charity)". Never translate anything into a language other than English. Do not drop or paraphrase the Arabic terms away.

## Key points

Bullet the main points discussed, in order. Apply the same rule: Arabic term in Latin letters, English meaning in brackets on first use.

## Decisions

List each decision made and who made it. If none, write "None".

## Action items

| Owner | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|

One row per action. Use names from the transcript. Write "Not stated" for a missing due date.

## Glossary of Arabic terms

| Term | Meaning | Where it was used |
|---|---|---|

One row for every Arabic or Islamic term that appeared in the transcript: the term in Latin letters, its closest English meaning, and the sentence it appeared in. If the transcript rendered a term in Arabic script, give the Latin spelling as well.
